Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask before starting.

Another question? Write to me and I will answer myself.

Talk to Marcos
What happens after I pay?

The invoice arrives by email and, once the payment is done, you see a page to book your set-up session. In that hour of video call we get your business running.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Once installed on your tablet, phone or computer, the app opens and works offline: taking orders, charging, sending kitchen tickets. When the network returns, it syncs. Internet is only needed to sync between devices and for bookings and orders coming from the web.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and in your own free Firebase project (Google). We create it together in the session. That way the data is yours and GastroGoan neither pays nor charges anything for storing it.

How many people can use it?

Your whole team, at no cost per user or per device. Each employee logs in with their PIN and sees only their area.

What if I have more than one venue?

Each venue has its own licence. You can open branches that inherit your menu, recipes, ingredients and protocols, and see them all from your same account.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. It is designed for the day-to-day of a venue. In the first session we set it up with you and you use it with your team before hanging up.

What if the internet drops in the middle of service?

You keep working. The app runs locally and syncs when the connection is back. Orders and bookings that come through the web wait until you recover it.

How do I get my menu and products in?

In the first session we load your menu, products and suppliers with you. If you have them in spreadsheets, we help you transfer them.

Does it update?

Yes, on its own and at no cost. If you are working it does not interrupt you: it tells you and you choose when to update.

Is the payment secure?

Stripe takes the payment, one of the most widely used payment processors in the world. GastroGoan never sees or stores your card details.

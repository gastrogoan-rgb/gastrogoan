Le Petit Comité. One table, many stories.

Private gastronomic events, guided tastings and chef at home. We take care of everything —menu, shopping, set-up, service and clean-up— so that you only have to be at the table.

Request a proposal

What we do

Three ways to sit at the table.

Service 01

Gastronomic events

Design and delivery of private celebrations, pop-ups and themed dinners: tailored menu, shopping and logistics, set-up and professional service. Adaptable to any format and capacity, with full coordination.

Service 02

Tastings and pairings

Guided sessions to discover products and combinations —wines, beers, oils, cheeses—, with sample selection, tasting script and support material. For small groups and loyalty activities.

Service 03

Private chef

Not a one-off event: I cook in your own home every week or every month, with the shopping done and meals ready, so you eat well without thinking about it. Also available for a specific day if you prefer.

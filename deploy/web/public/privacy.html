What data we process and why

If you write to us by WhatsApp, email or Instagram: your name, phone or email and whatever you tell us. To reply and, if you ask, to prepare a proposal or quote.

If you buy the App: the billing details you enter on the payment page (name or company, tax ID, address, email). To set you up, send you your login, issue the invoice and provide the service. Card details are handled directly by Stripe and never reach us.

If you book advisory or an event: the data needed for the proposal, the service and the invoice.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising or sell it. We do not make automated decisions about you.

Legal basis

Replying and preparing a proposal: your request (pre-contractual steps, Art. 6.1.b GDPR). Providing the service you buy: the contract (Art. 6.1.b). Invoicing and bookkeeping: legal obligation (Art. 6.1.c).

How long we keep it

Messages, for as long as the conversation lasts and up to one year afterwards if no contract follows. Customer data, for the duration of the relationship and then for as long as commercial and tax law requires (up to 6 years).

Who else sees it (processors and third parties)

Only the providers we need to operate: Cloudflare (hosting of this website), Stripe (payments), Google (email and the licence platform database) and Meta (WhatsApp and Instagram, if you contact us there). Some are based in the United States; transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Your accountant or the Spanish Tax Agency will receive billing data where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to gastrogoan@gmail.com. If you believe we are not handling your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or store anything in your browser to track you or show you ads, which is why there is no cookie banner. Fonts are served from this same website.

If we measure visits, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and identifies no one. Tapping a WhatsApp, Instagram or payment button takes you to those services, which have their own cookie policies.

Data you keep in the App

Your business and customer data (bookings, sales, staff…) is stored in your own Firebase project, in your Google account. You are the controller of that data. What we do with the part that goes through our platform is set out in the terms of purchase.
